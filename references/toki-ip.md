# Toki IP Rules

Toki is a short, chubby, head-body-integrated triangular pear-shaped yellow mascot. The IP proportions are fixed; the poster role is not fixed.

Use `assets/toki-three-view-reference.png` as the visual reference whenever possible. The asset is a proportion guide, not a fixed pose template.

## Must Keep

- Body width close to 80%-90% of total height.
- Bottom visibly wider than top.
- Legs extremely short, thick, and rounded, about 10%-15% of total height.
- Arms short and rounded; never long.
- Huge eyes.
- Red round nose.
- Small mouth.
- Top curved crest.
- Short round limbs.
- Bright yellow body.

## Theme Freedom

Choose Toki's action, prop, clothing, and location from the current campaign theme:

- sales champion: crown, small trophy, laurel badge, leaderboard card,
- 618/e-commerce: small `618` badge, restrained coupon ribbon, shopping medal,
- summer sprint: sun visor, speed ribbon, rocket badge,
- target hit: medal, flag, target marker,
- onboarding: welcome sign, small suitcase, handshake pose.

Do not reuse the previous poster's Toki pose by default. Re-decide Toki's role for each new campaign. Same-month unified layout may keep the same Toki role.

## Scale and Placement

- Toki is a supporting mascot, not the main subject unless explicitly requested.
- Toki should reinforce the theme and guide attention toward the title, portrait, or achievement copy.
- Avoid placing Toki where it blocks the face, name, team, or personal title.
- If the design is already busy, make Toki smaller and simpler.

## Must Avoid

- Tall or slim bean shape.
- Human-like doll body.
- Long limbs.
- Narrow waist.
- Realistic animal or ordinary cartoon person.
- Stretching the mascot to fit a layout.
- Reusing a fixed pose/prop across unrelated themes.

## Prompt Snippet

Use this in ImageGen prompts whenever Toki appears:
