# Toki IP Rules

Toki is a short, chubby, head-body-integrated triangular pear-shaped yellow mascot.

## Must Keep

- Body width close to 80%-90% of total height.
- Bottom visibly wider than top.
- Legs extremely short, thick, and rounded, about 10%-15% of total height.
- Arms short and rounded; never long.
- Huge eyes.
- Red round nose.
- Small mouth.
- Top curved crest.
- Short round limbs.
- Bright yellow body.

## Can Change

- Pose.
- Facial expression.
- Clothing.
- Props.
- Theme action, such as holding a trophy, flag, medal, megaphone, rocket, ribbon, or sales badge.

## Must Avoid

- Tall or slim bean shape.
- Human-like doll body.
- Long limbs.
- Narrow waist.
- Realistic animal or ordinary cartoon person.
- Stretching the mascot to fit a layout.

## Prompt Snippet

Use this in ImageGen prompts whenever Toki appears:

`Toki must strictly keep the original IP proportions: short chubby head-body-integrated triangular pear shape, body width 80%-90% of height, bottom wider than top, extremely short thick rounded legs only 10%-15% of height, short rounded arms, huge eyes, red round nose, small mouth, top curved crest, bright yellow body. Do not make Toki tall, slim, bean-shaped, humanoid, or long-limbed.`
